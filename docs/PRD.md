# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Dual-Side Laser Hazard Marker (BX) System

### 1. Product Overview
The Dual-Side Laser Hazard Marker (BX) System is an independent, automated safety mechanism designed to project high-visibility cross-laser warning lines across road hazards and potholes in India, preventing nighttime accidents.

### 2. Problem Statement
Road surface hazards and unlit potholes lead to severe vehicle damage and fatal accidents at night, primarily due to lack of early visual warnings for drivers.

### 3. Goals
- Provide an automated, low-cost safety warning solution for road hazards.
- Project cross-lasers from both sides to clearly demarcate the danger zone.
- Ensure low power consumption suitable for continuous roadside deployment.

### 4. Target Users
- Local commuters, night drivers, and two-wheeler riders on Indian roads.
- Municipal bodies or local safety authorities looking for smart road tools.

### 5. Core Features (MVP)
- Real-time hazard zone detection simulation.
- Dual-side independent laser unit activation control.
- Low-power standby and high-visibility alert modes.
